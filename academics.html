Beyond the Classroom

Clubs & Activities

🏅

Sports Club

Football, volleyball, athletics, and table tennis. Annual inter-house sports day draws the whole community together.

📖

Qur'an Club

Weekly Tajweed lessons, hifz circles, and preparation for state and national Qur'an recitation competitions.

🔬

Science Club

Lab experiments, science fair projects, and guest lectures from professionals inspire young scientists every week.

🗣️

Debate & Quiz Society

Sharpens critical thinking and public speaking. Our teams have won Kwara State debate competitions three years running.

🎨

Arts & Craft Club

Calligraphy, Islamic geometric art, painting, and drama — nurturing creativity inspired by Islamic heritage.

💻

Coding Club

Introductory programming (Scratch & Python) for primary and JSS pupils. Robotics projects underway.
