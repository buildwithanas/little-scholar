Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What age can my child start at Little Scholar? +

We accept children from age 1 into our creche class. Primary school starts from age 6. All prospective students must sit our entrance assessment (except Nursery applicants).

Is the school open to non-Muslim students? +

Yes. The school is open to both Muslim and non-Muslim students.

Do you offer school bus services? +

No, the school does not currently offer school bus services. However, we encourage parents and guardians to make suitable transportation arrangements that ensure the safety and punctuality of their children. The school remains committed to maintaining a convenient schedule to support families with drop-off and pick-up.

When are school fees due? +

School fees are due one week before or on the first day of each new term. A 5% discount is given for payment before the resumption date. Payment can be made by bank transfer or directly at the bursary.

How do I get my child's result PIN? +

Result PINs are distributed at the end of each term along with your child's printed report card. If you have lost your PIN, visit the school bursary with a valid ID and your child's registration number.
