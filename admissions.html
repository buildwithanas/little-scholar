How to Apply

Simple Steps to Enrolment

1
Purchase Admission Form
Visit the school's bursary or collect from the front desk. Forms cost ₦3,000 (non-refundable).
2
Complete & Submit the Form
Fill in all sections accurately and attach required documents. Submit at the admissions office.
3
Sit the Entrance Examination
All applicants (Primary 1 and above) sit a short assessment in English, Mathematics, and Islamic Studies.
4
Receive Admission Letter
Successful candidates are notified within 5 working days. Proceed to pay acceptance fees and collect uniform.
📋 Required Documents
  • ✔ Birth Certificate or Declaration of Age
  • ✔ Previous School's Last Term Result (for transfers)
  • ✔ Transfer Certificate (for transfer students)
  • ✔ 4 Recent Passport Photographs (white background)
  • ✔ Parent/Guardian's Valid ID (NIN slip or Driver's Licence)
  • ✔ Completed Admission Form
  • ✔ Evidence of payment (bank teller / receipt)

Financial Aid

Scholarship Programme

We believe no brilliant child should be denied quality education due to financial hardship. Our annual scholarship programme awards partial and full bursaries to deserving students.

Eligible candidates are selected on the basis of academic performance, financial need, and demonstrated Islamic character. Applications open every July.
