Our History

Eighteen Years of Faithful Service

Little Scholar Islamic School was founded in 2005. Their dream was simple but profound: to build a school where children could receive a first-class education without sacrificing their Islamic identity.

Beginning with just 80 pupils and 6 teachers in a rented building, the school has grown into a full-fledged institution spanning Nursery, Primary, and Junior Secondary levels, accommodating over 800 students annually.

Today, Little Scholar alumni are found in top federal government colleges, universities, and professional careers across Nigeria and beyond — each one carrying the twin gifts of knowledge and faith.

Our Foundation

Mission, Vision & Core Values

🎯

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, value-based education that nurtures intellectually competent, morally upright, and spiritually grounded Muslim scholars capable of serving humanity.

🌟

Our Vision

To be the leading Islamic school in West Africa, producing graduates who are leaders in every field while remaining anchored in the Qur'an and Sunnah.

💎

Core Values

Taqwa (God-consciousness), Ilm (Knowledge), Adab (Character), Itqan (Excellence), Ukhuwwah (Brotherhood & Sisterhood).
