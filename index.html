Our Mission

Rooted in Faith,
Built for the Future

At Little Scholar Islamic School, we believe every child is a trust from Allah (SWT). Our integrated curriculum blends rigorous academics with the moral framework of Islam — Qur'an, Tafsir, Arabic, and Islamic Studies sit alongside Mathematics, Science, English, and Technology.

Our graduates consistently excel in external examinations (Common Entrance, BECE, WAEC) while embodying the character of a true Muslim scholar.
